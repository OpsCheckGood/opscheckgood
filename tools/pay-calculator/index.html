  • Basic pay only. Housing and subsistence allowances, special and incentive pays, and taxes are not included.
  • The E-1 rate applies after 4 months of active duty; an E-1 with less than 4 months is paid $2,225.70.
  • O-1, O-2 and O-3 rates do not apply to commissioned officers credited with over 4 years (at least 4 years and 1 day) of active duty service as an enlisted member or warrant officer; those officers are paid at the O-1E, O-2E and O-3E rates.
  • Basic pay for O-7 through O-10 is limited to $18,999.90 a month (Executive Schedule level II) and for O-6 and below to $15,408.30 (level V) during calendar year 2026.
  • Basic pay rate for Academy cadets and midshipmen and ROTC members is $1,452.90.
