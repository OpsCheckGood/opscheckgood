5Review

Nothing flagged.

  • A5.1.4 · The inclusive period stays on one line.
  • A5.1.5 · "long and" in a retirement closing is for 30 or more years of service. Air National Guard members not on extended active duty take the Guard closing.
  • A5.1.6 · No abbreviations other than Jr., Sr., II, III after the name; spell out the grade in the opening sentence, then use the short title. Code names such as RED FLAG are fine. Never split the member's name across two lines.
  • A5.1.7 · Numeric unit designators read 3rd Aerospace Wing, 4th Mission Support Group.
  • A5.1.10 · Numbers through nine are spelled out unless space is limited.
  • A5.1.14 · Only abbreviations and acronyms on the approved DAF list.
